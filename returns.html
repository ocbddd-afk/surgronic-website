Returns & Claims

Prevention over returns — we get it right before it ships.

7-Day Inspection Window

You have 7 days from delivery to inspect your instruments. If anything does not match the confirmed specification, contact us immediately with photos.

Our Prevention Process

Every order follows: written Specification Confirmation (your sign-off) → deposit → pre-shipment inspection photos/video (your approval) → shipment. Nothing ships until you've seen it and approved it.

Claims

If an instrument arrives not matching the confirmed specification:

  • Notify us within 7 days with photos
  • We arrange return or replacement
  • Refund or credit issued upon verification

Cancellations

Deposit refundable minus costs already committed to the supplier, stated plainly in every quotation.
